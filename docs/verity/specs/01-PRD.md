## Product decision

**Vision.** Help a writer move from a private draft to an evidence-backed decision in one workspace: **CREATE → VERIFY → PROVE**. The product must make its source coverage, uncertainty, version lineage and human decisions visible. The business objective is a trustworthy, commercially usable writing and document platform, not a detector-evasion service or an unqualified plagiarism judgment.

**Exactly five product paths:** Azaeron AI, AI Humaniser, AI Detector, Plagiarism Checker and Documents. Home, History, Settings, Help, reports, citations, authorship and provenance are supporting navigation and evidence views within those paths, not additional products. The existing desktop application and first-party API remain the delivery surfaces.

**Primary users and jobs.** (1) A student or professional writer wants to improve a draft without losing its claims, citations or voice. (2) A researcher wants a version-bound answer with inspectable document evidence. (3) An editor wants reversible, selective changes and a durable audit trail. (4) An educator or reviewer wants limited detection and overlap evidence without an automated misconduct verdict. (5) A workspace administrator wants scoped access, quotas, deletion and verifiable service behavior. These are design personas, not measured market segments; customer discovery is still needed before commercial positioning is asserted.

**Competitive position, evidence boundary.** Grammarly documents context-aware compose/rewrite and personal-voice controls; Phrasly documents paste/import, rewrite modes and draft alternatives; Turnitin distinguishes AI-writing signals from similarity and warns against using AI detection alone for adverse action; Copyleaks documents distinct detection and plagiarism API products. These are vendor-described behaviors, not head-to-head measurements. Azaeron's intended distinction is an independently checked candidate, exact source/version provenance and an explicit accept decision in a private workspace. No superiority claim is approved until the sealed bake-off in PRD-021 passes. Sources: [Grammarly support](https://support.grammarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/14528857014285-Introducing-generative-AI-assistance), [Phrasly workflow](https://help.phrasly.ai/en/articles/8590142-how-to-humanize-your-first-document), [Turnitin AI report](https://guides.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/22774058814093-Using-the-AI-Writing-Report), [Turnitin similarity](https://guides.turnitin.com/hc/en-us/articles/23435833938701-Understanding-the-similarity-score), [Copyleaks API products](https://docs.copyleaks.com/concepts/products/overview).

## Commercial and measurement contract

Proposed product KPIs are **targets, not results**: task completion ≥90% for each of the five moderated core journeys; critical protected-fact error rate 0 on the sealed release set; verified-answer support precision ≥95% on adjudicated passages; abstention on unsupported document questions ≥95%; no tenant leak in hostile tests; and measured p95 revision save/conflict ≤1,000 ms at concurrency eight on a representative deployment. Methods: blinded task observation, adjudicated answer/source labels, red-team corpus, and the existing load harness with frozen input/hardware descriptors. Detector thresholds and acceptable subgroup gaps must be predeclared from CAL, then measured on untouched TEST/OOD; no number is selected after seeing TEST.

Business signals after a lawful pilot are activation (first completed document action), repeat use (second accepted edit or evidence review), successful source inspection, and support burden per 100 active workspaces. Instrument only consented/necessary event metadata; never put customer text into telemetry. No revenue, retention or market-share assertion is available today.

**Non-goals:** detector-score evasion, automated academic misconduct decisions, Internet-wide similarity without an indexed licensed corpus, public GPU deployment from the Mac preview, unsupported modalities, a sixth product, and replacement of the current Next.js/FastAPI/PostgreSQL/RLS/Redis/MinIO architecture without measured justification. Paid billing, SDKs and enterprise SSO are not implied by existing tables or screens.

Commercial release requires all PRD-006/007/013/014/021/022 gates, legal/privacy rights approval, independent reviewers, production runtime attestation, representative security/load/recovery/accessibility evidence and an accountable release decision. Until then the explicit verdict is **NOT PRODUCTION READY**.
