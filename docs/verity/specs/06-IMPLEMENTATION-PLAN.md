## Execution rule and review discipline

**Definition of Ready for material work:** PRD/TRD IDs and testable acceptance are written; owner role and data/rights boundary are identified; threat/tenant/rollback impact is understood; necessary external authorization exists. A small corrective patch may use a linked issue and concise test without inventing a new PRD ID.

**Definition of Done:** source, API/schema/UI/worker changes form one usable vertical slice; unit/integration/browser tests relevant to the change pass; auth/RLS/quota/idempotency and privacy are reviewed; OpenAPI/migration/spec snapshots are updated intentionally; telemetry excludes content; evidence is linked in the matrix; rollback and docs are updated; hosted exact-commit CI has a recorded result. A blocked external dependency is not “done.”

**PR review checklist:** confirm requirement/acceptance, behavior before/after, tenant and API-key authorization, hostile input/prompt-injection and private egress, data rights/PII and retention, concurrency/cancellation/retry/idempotency, provenance and fail-closed states, keyboard/assistive technology and mobile states, API/schema/migration compatibility, meaningful tests, image/dependency/SBOM status, rollback and evidence. The [PR template](../../../.github/pull_request_template.md) carries the short form. Engineering reviewers must not self-certify owner rights decisions, blinded human scores or production model approval.

**Authority:** engineering may implement and measure within local resources. The Azaeron Verity project owner is the one final accountable internal approver for commercial dataset/training rights, base-model derivative use, dataset provenance/privacy/copyright and checkpoint release. The owner also authorizes paid GPU spend. The owner reviews a hash-bound decision record; no approval is inferred from code, role or silence. The owner may obtain legal/privacy advice but no extra internal signature layer is required. Third-party rights, applicable law and contracts remain binding; unresolved evidence fails the gate. At least two independent humans per item provide blinded quality judgments and cannot be replaced by the owner. The current approved production-model count is **zero**.
