## Exact first-tranche request

Acquire **720 source→human-written-target pairs from 12 distinct, identified rights holders**, 60 pairs per creator-editor. Each person supplies original source drafts and underlying documents and personally writes the target; a second person independently checks quality. No fabricated authors, signatures, professional credentials, paid work or ratings. Arrange at least four contributors experienced in professional writing and four in academic writing. Contributors and reviewers must be adults and must not submit customer, student, employer-confidential, medical or other sensitive third-party content. A contributor may supply fictional but internally consistent situations; “fictional” must be labeled and cannot support factual-world claims.

At least 18 of each contributor's 60 rows deliberately contain protected names, dates, numbers, quotations, citations, negation or qualifiers, with marked source spans and expected invariant checks; this yields at least **216** invariant-rich examples. Citation claims must point to supplied source material; no invented bibliography. The targets must be actual human work and carry an explicit `human_written_target` label. If an AI draft is later used as a source, it requires a separately identified model/output-rights record and must never be relabeled human authored.

Allocate eight contributors to TRAIN; one different contributor each to VAL, CAL, TEST and OOD. TRAIN includes professional and academic contributors. The single TEST contributor must have independently verified experience in both professional and academic editing and supply original examples in both domains; otherwise recruit an additional TEST contributor and increase the tranche rather than mislabel the coverage. Reserve a technical-procedure domain for OOD and keep that domain and contributor out of TRAIN/VAL/CAL/TEST. Assign all rows sharing a source, editor, author, document, passage or derived version to the same split. Before sealing, run exact and near-duplicate checks on **both input and target** and all source-group identifiers using the existing `backend/app/modules/model_platform/datasets.py` reader; reject overlaps rather than reassigning a contaminated held-out item. These counts are acquisition targets, not a claim that every commissioned row will pass review. Rejected rows must be replaced before constructing the five splits.

## Rights, provenance and privacy terms to obtain before collecting text

The signed or otherwise authenticated contributor agreement must identify the actual rights holder and bind its version, grant ID, contributor/author ID, document IDs, source and target SHA-256, signature/assent evidence, date and withdrawal channel. Its proposed operative grant is:

> I authored or control the listed source documents, drafts and human-edited targets. I grant Azaeron Verity a non-exclusive, worldwide right to store, copy, transform and use only these identified materials to develop, train, validate, calibrate and evaluate Azaeron AI models and to commercially serve resulting model weights and outputs. I permit confidential service providers to process them solely for that purpose. I do not grant public redistribution of my raw text. I have identified every third-party work and personal-data element; anything not separately licensed or lawfully usable for this scope must be excluded before submission.

The final instrument must explicitly address compensation or volunteer participation, assignment versus license where applicable, attribution preference, contributor withdrawal, model-checkpoint withdrawal consequences, and governing retention/privacy notice. **This proposed language is not a signed grant or a legal finding.** A blanket repository license, public posting or employment relationship is not substituted for the appropriate rights-holder grant. Where source and editor are different people, obtain and link both permissions. Exclude copied web text, proprietary references, undisclosed AI outputs, customer material and quotations without a recorded compatible source license and task permission. A copyright reviewer must inspect each row and its underlying document, including unflagged rows; automated matching is only triage.

Primary-law review inputs, not a certification: [India's Copyright Act, 1957, sections 30 and 30A](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1367/1/a195714.pdf) address owner-granted written licenses and their terms. The [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/22037/2/a2023-22.pdf) addresses notice, purpose and withdrawal where applicable; the [2025 Rules Gazette](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf) provides staggered commencement dates. Applicable jurisdiction, commencement, lawful basis and exact agreement language require accountable legal/privacy review; this brief does not make those determinations.

Proposed collection controls requiring the owner's privacy/legal approval before intake: encrypted, access-limited private storage; no raw text in Git, telemetry or external AI APIs; a pseudonymous row index in review artifacts; automated PII/secrets/URL scanning followed by human inspection; removal or substitution before acceptance of identifying data unless a documented lawful basis and necessity exist. Proposed retention is **30 days for rejected raw submissions**, **12 months for accepted raw data with renewal only by reviewed decision**, and **30 days maximum in encrypted backups after deletion**, with a hash-only deletion/tombstone record retained long enough to prevent restore. A request to remove already trained content triggers checkpoint impact review, retirement/retraining where required and an honest response; “machine unlearning” is not promised. These are requested contract terms, not current operating policy or legal certification.

## Accountable review and effort

One source author/editor checks the original and target; a separate copyright/privacy reviewer checks every accepted row and source, signed grants and flagged/unflagged PII. A separate data-quality editor checks grammar, naturalness, source fidelity, voice and invariant labels. The project owner then signs a hash-bound dataset decision **only after** these reviews and the five-way leakage audit pass. For the sealed TEST and OOD items, two independent blinded reviewers per item must rate actual model outputs without seeing model identity; the author and this agent do not act as substitute reviewers.

Planning estimate, **not a spend authorization or procurement quote**: 720 human source/edit pairs at 15–25 minutes each = **180–300 person-hours**; 72 source documents at 15–30 minutes = **18–36 hours**; independent rights/privacy/quality review at 5–8 minutes per pair = **60–96 hours**; two blinded ratings of 120 sealed items at 5–8 minutes each = **20–32 hours**. Total **278–464 person-hours**, excluding recruiting, revisions, legal review and model evaluation. Fees are **unquoted and not authorized**. A smaller or lower-quality set may test mechanics but cannot support the planned five-split quality claim.
